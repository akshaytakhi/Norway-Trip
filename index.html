Things first-timers miss

Pro tips for peak Norway.

📱
Apps to install before flying: Ruter (Oslo), Skyss Billett (Bergen), Vy (national trains/buses), AutoPASS Visitors (toll roads if driving), Yr (weather).
💳
Cards beat cash everywhere. Carry one Visa + one Mastercard. ATMs are scarce in fjord villages. Many tolls auto-bill the rental — don't pay cash.
🌦
June weather is bipolar: 22°C in Oslo, 6°C on Loen Skylift summit, sideways rain in Bergen 50% of days. Pack 3 layers + waterproof jacket + light gloves.
☀
Daylight runs ~03:30 to ~23:30. Bring eye-mask. Don't skip rest just because it's still bright at 22:00.
🛂
Schengen visa covers all of Norway. Carry hard-copy printout to show on departure. E-Arrival India card required within 72 hrs before Jun 19 return.
📡
SIM: Pick up Telia or Telenor prepaid at OSL on arrival, ~NOK 200 for 10 GB. Or use roaming if cheap.
⛽
Driving in fjord country: speed limits enforced, toll plates auto-charge, ferries take cards on board, fuel ~NOK 22/litre. Drive defensively on hairpin roads.
